Spam Word Cloud

Identifies the most frequent stemmed terms across spam messages. Words like "call", "free", "txt", and "text" appear with high density.

Top 15 Keywords Frequency

Histogram of the top 15 terms in spam messages. It helps identify high-importance feature tokens for the Naive Bayes and SVM models.

Performance Benchmarking

Compares classification accuracy across Multinomial Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, and Support Vector Machine. The highest accuracy model is automatically serialized and served.

Error Rates Heatmap (Best Model)

Shows True Positives, False Positives (Ham classified as Spam), True Negatives, and False Negatives. In spam classification, minimizing False Positives (misclassifying important emails) is critical.

Class Balance Check

Displays the ratio of legitimate (ham) to spam messages in the original SMS Spam Collection corpus. High imbalance explains why F1-score is preferred over pure accuracy.
